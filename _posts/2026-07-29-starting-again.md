This site has been around for a while, mostly as a compact record of projects, papers, and past work. I wanted it to become something more useful: a place where I can share what I am learning while I build.

My work tends to live at the intersection of machine learning, systems, and product. That intersection is full of questions that do not fit neatly into a project README or a research abstract:

- How do you know when a promising model is ready to become a dependable system?
- Which parts of an ML problem deserve more sophistication—and which deserve less?
- What changes when an experiment meets real data, real constraints, and real users?
- How can we make complex technical ideas easier to reason about?

Those are the kinds of questions I plan to explore here. Some posts will be technical deep dives. Others will be shorter field notes: a useful pattern, a paper worth revisiting, an experiment that failed in an interesting way, or an idea that became clearer after writing it down.

I also want this site to remain a living map of my work. The projects and research pages collect the public artifacts; the writing will capture more of the thinking behind them.

If any of that sounds useful, check back occasionally—or say hello. I am always happy to compare notes.
